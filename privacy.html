Privacy Policy — Boards to Bedside

Last Updated: August 10, 2026
The short version: Boards to Bedside does not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share any of your personal data. Everything you do in the app stays on your device. There are no accounts, no servers, no analytics, no ads, and no tracking of any kind.

Who We Are

Boards to Bedside is a board-exam preparation app for nurse practitioners, operated by Between Breaths LLC. Questions about this policy? Contact the email at the bottom of this page.

What Data the App Stores

The app saves your study data so you can pick up where you left off: your answers to practice questions, progress, scores and streaks, review history, and settings such as your theme. All of this is stored only on your own device, in your browser's or app's local storage. It is never sent anywhere. We cannot see it, access it, or recover it — it exists only on your device.

What We Collect

Nothing. Boards to Bedside has:

  • No user accounts and no sign-up
  • No servers of our own — nothing is uploaded or synced
  • No analytics or usage tracking
  • No advertising
  • No third-party SDKs or trackers
  • No collection of names, email addresses, locations, device identifiers, or any other personal information

All study content is bundled with the app itself, so the app works without sending your activity anywhere.

Web Version, Hosting, and Fonts

The web version is served by a third-party static web host. As with any website, that host's servers may automatically log standard access data such as your IP address for security and operational purposes. This is handled by the host, not by us, and we never receive or review it. The web version also loads its display fonts from Google Fonts, which likewise receives a standard web request (including your IP address) when fonts load. The installed mobile app runs entirely from content stored on your device and does not need to contact any server to work.

Deleting Your Data

Because all data lives on your device, you are in full control: deleting the app (or clearing your browser's site data for Boards to Bedside) deletes all of your data permanently. There is no copy anywhere else.

Children's Privacy

Boards to Bedside is intended for nursing students and healthcare professionals. Since the app collects no data from anyone, it does not collect data from children.

Changes to This Policy

If the app's data practices ever change — for example, if a future version adds optional accounts or sync — this policy will be updated before those changes take effect, and the "Last Updated" date above will be revised.
