Privacy Policy — Pass My Boards

Last Updated: August 8, 2026
The short version: Pass My Boards does not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share any of your personal data. Everything you do in the app stays on your device. There are no accounts, no servers, no analytics, no ads, and no tracking of any kind.

Who We Are

Pass My Boards is a board-exam preparation app for nurse practitioner and nursing students, created and operated by Danny DeStefano. Questions about this policy? Contact the email at the bottom of this page.

What Data the App Stores

The app saves your study data so you can pick up where you left off: your answers to practice questions, progress, scores and streaks, review history, and settings such as your theme. All of this is stored only on your own device, in your browser's or app's local storage. It is never sent anywhere. We cannot see it, access it, or recover it — it exists only on your device.

What We Collect

Nothing. Pass My Boards has:

  • No user accounts and no sign-up
  • No servers of our own — nothing is uploaded or synced
  • No analytics or usage tracking
  • No advertising
  • No third-party SDKs or trackers
  • No collection of names, email addresses, locations, device identifiers, or any other personal information

All study content is bundled with the app itself, so the app works without sending your activity anywhere.

Web Version, Hosting, and Fonts

The web version is hosted on GitHub Pages. When you load the website, GitHub's servers may automatically log standard web-server access data, such as your IP address, for security and operational purposes — this is standard for any website and is handled by GitHub, not by us; we never see it. See the GitHub Privacy Statement. The web version also loads its display fonts from Google Fonts, which similarly receives a standard web request (including your IP address) when fonts load. The installed mobile app runs entirely from content stored on your device.

Deleting Your Data

Because all data lives on your device, you are in full control: deleting the app (or clearing your browser's site data for Pass My Boards) deletes all of your data permanently. There is no copy anywhere else.

Children's Privacy

Pass My Boards is intended for nursing students and healthcare professionals. Since the app collects no data from anyone, it does not collect data from children.

Changes to This Policy

If the app's data practices ever change — for example, if a future version adds optional accounts or sync — this policy will be updated before those changes take effect, and the "Last Updated" date above will be revised.
